You're invited to try WeatherBoy

Live Tampa Bay and Florida radar, a storm loop that shows storms moving, lightning, 3D rainfall, a 24-hour forecast and plain-language safety guides. It installs on your phone like an app, for free.

Open WeatherBoy ▶ Or open the Storm Loop app
✅ WeatherBoy is installed. Open it from your home screen.

🍎 iPhone or iPad Your device

  1. Open this page in Safari (the compass icon). In other apps, tap ⋯ or Share and choose Open in Safari.
  2. Tap Open WeatherBoy above.
  3. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  5. Open WeatherBoy from its new icon. It runs full-screen, like any app.

🤖 Android Your device

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install WeatherBoy above if you see it. Otherwise tap ⋮ (top right) → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open WeatherBoy from your home screen or app drawer.

💻 Computer Your device

Want it on your phone? Point your phone's camera at this code and follow the steps for your phone.

To use it on this computer, open it in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

🧪 What to try

  • Live radar · pick a radar, switch Reflectivity / Velocity, tap the LIVE badge to refresh.
  • Storm Loop · press play and watch the past hour; drag the bar to step through scans.
  • Home page · current weather, storms and lightning near Tampa, tides, UV and the 7-day.
  • Forecast · the 24-hour charts and the hour-by-hour strip.
  • Be safe and educated · the guides to watches vs. warnings, radar colors, CAPE and more.
  • Units · switch between °F / mph and °C / km/h on the home page.

Notice something odd, slow or confusing? Let whoever sent you this link know: what you tapped, what you expected, and your phone model.

📌 Good to know

  • This is a beta: things may change or occasionally break while it's being improved.
  • WeatherBoy updates itself. To get the newest version, fully close the app and open it again.
  • It's free, with no sign-up, ads or tracking. Weather data loads straight from public sources such as NOAA.
  • WeatherBoy is not an official warning service. In dangerous weather, follow National Weather Service warnings and local officials.
